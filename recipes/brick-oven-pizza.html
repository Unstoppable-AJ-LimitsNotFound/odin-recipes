Brick-Oven Pizza

Description

This Brooklyn‑style brick‑oven pizza is inspired by classic wood‑fire pizzerias in New York. The crust is thin, crisp, and chewy, with a subtle flavor from a long overnight rise. Topped simply with mozzarella, crushed tomatoes, olive oil, oregano, pepper, and fresh basil, it delivers that authentic pizzeria taste right from your home oven. Best enjoyed hot with a cold lager.

Ingredients

Dough:

  • ¼ cup warm water
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 1 cup cold water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups bread flour
  • 1 tablespoon extra‑virgin olive oil

Toppings:

    6 ounces low‑moisture mozzarella cheese, thinly sliced
  • ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ cup no‑salt‑added canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons extra‑virgin olive oil
  • 6 fresh basil leaves, torn

Steps:

  1. Pour warm water into a large bowl and sprinkle yeast over the top. Let stand for 5 minutes. Stir in cold water and salt. Gradually add flour, 1 cup at a time, until incorporated.
  2. Knead dough on a floured surface until smooth, about 10 minutes.
  3. Divide into two balls, coat with olive oil, and refrigerate in a sealed container for at least 16 hours.
  4. Remove dough from the refrigerator one hour before baking.
  5. Preheat oven with a pizza stone on the lowest rack to 550°F (288°C). Lightly dust a pizza peel with flour.
  6. Stretch one dough ball into a 14‑inch round and place on the peel.
  7. Top with half the mozzarella, oregano, pepper, crushed tomatoes, and olive oil.
  8. Slide pizza onto the stone and bake until crust begins to brown, 4–6 minutes.
  9. Remove from oven, sprinkle with half the basil, slice, and serve.
  10. Repeat with the second dough ball for the second pizza.
