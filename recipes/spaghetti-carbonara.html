Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti Carbonara is a classic Roman pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, pancetta, and pepper. It’s creamy, savory, and comforting, with a silky sauce that clings to every strand of pasta. Perfect for a quick yet indulgent meal.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • 4 ounces pancetta or bacon, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain.
  2. In a skillet, cook pancetta until crisp. Add garlic and sauté briefly.
  3. Whisk eggs and Parmesan together in a bowl.
  4. Add hot pasta to the skillet, remove from heat, and quickly stir in egg mixture.
  5. Add reserved pasta water as needed to create a creamy sauce.
  6. Season generously with black pepper and serve immediately.
